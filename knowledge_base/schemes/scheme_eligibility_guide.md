# Government scheme eligibility (India — awareness guide)

## PM-KISAN
- ₹6,000 per year in three installments for eligible land-holding farmer families.
- Requires valid land records and Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## PMFBY (crop insurance)
- Protects against notified risks; enroll before cutoff for each season/crop/area.
- Premium subsidized for farmers.

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- Seasonal credit for crop production and allied activities.
- Interest subvention when repaid on time.

## State schemes
- Vary by state: investment support per acre, input subsidies, horticulture missions.
- Check state agriculture department portal for district notifications.
