# Crop Insurance (PMFBY) - Farmer FAQ

## Claim after rainfall / flood damage
1. Inform insurance company and bank within 72 hours of loss
2. Do not uproot damaged crop until survey officer inspection
3. Photograph field with date stamp; note GPS if possible
4. Submit claim form, land records, sowing certificate, Aadhaar

## Documents required
- Insurance policy / receipt
- Panchayat damage certificate (if applicable)
- Bank account details for settlement

## Timeline
- Survey within 15 days of intimation
- Settlement typically 30-45 days after survey approval

## GreenHarvest support
- Field officer can help complete claim form
- Escalate disputed survey to district agriculture officer
