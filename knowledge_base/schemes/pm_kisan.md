# PM-KISAN and Government Scheme Information

## PM-KISAN
- Rs 6000 per year in three installments for eligible landholding farmers
- Check status on PM-KISAN portal with Aadhaar-linked bank account
- eKYC updates required if payment pending

## State subsidy schemes
- Seed subsidy through authorized dealers only
- Fertilizer subsidy via POS machines - keep receipts

## Application support
- Visit nearest CSC or agriculture office with land passbook
- GreenHarvest village centers assist with form filling only - no fee
