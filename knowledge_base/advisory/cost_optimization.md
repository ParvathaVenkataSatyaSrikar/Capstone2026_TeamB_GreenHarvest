# Cost optimization for farmers

## Fertilizer
- Soil test before season — often saves 15–25% nitrogen cost.
- Use split application instead of single heavy dose.
- Compare subsidized POS dealer prices for urea/DAP.

## Pest management
- Scout weekly; spot spray instead of calendar blanket sprays.
- Generic molecules with same active ingredient may cost less than premium brands.

## Organic alternatives
- Compost and vermicompost can replace part of chemical fertilizer.
- Neem oil and bio-pesticides for early pest pressure in vegetables.
- Trichoderma and Pseudomonas for soil-borne disease reduction.

## Machinery and labour
- Custom hiring centres (CHC) for tractor operations reduce ownership cost.
- Line sowing and transplanting save seed and labour in paddy.
