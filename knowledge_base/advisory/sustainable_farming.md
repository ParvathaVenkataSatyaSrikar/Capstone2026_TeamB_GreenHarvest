# Sustainable farming practices

## Water conservation
- Prefer drip or sprinkler over flood irrigation where possible.
- Schedule irrigation using soil moisture or leaf appearance, not fixed calendar only.
- Harvest rainwater in farm ponds for supplemental irrigation.

## Soil preservation
- Add compost or FYM to maintain organic matter.
- Minimum tillage where erosion risk is high.
- Cover crops or mulching between rows in vegetables.

## Organic and IPM
- Use pheromone traps and neem-based products before broad-spectrum chemicals.
- Rotate crops to break pest and disease cycles.
- Maintain field sanitation — remove infected plant debris.

## Energy and inputs
- Group input purchases through FPO for efficiency.
- Solar pump sets reduce long-term diesel use where subsidized.
