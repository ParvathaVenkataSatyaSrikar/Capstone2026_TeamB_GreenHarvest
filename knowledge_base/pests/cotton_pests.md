# Cotton Pest Management Manual

## Whitefly
- Symptoms: yellowing, honeydew, sooty mold
- Action: yellow sticky traps, avoid broad-spectrum sprays early season
- Escalate if population doubles within one week

## Bollworm complex
- Monitor pheromone trap catches daily during flowering
- Economic threshold: consult local extension bulletin
- Emergency escalation for >5 larvae per 10 plants

## Outbreak indicators
- Multiple farmers in same mandal reporting same pest within 7 days
- Field officer visit recommended for cluster confirmation
