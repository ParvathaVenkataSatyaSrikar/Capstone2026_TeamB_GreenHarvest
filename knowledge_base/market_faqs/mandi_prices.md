# Market Price Assistance FAQ

## How to use mandi price data
- Prices are per quintal at local APMC - verify on arrival
- Compare trend: rising, stable, falling before bulk sale

## Tomato - Nashik region
- Peak season prices vary 1200-2500 INR/quintal
- Grade A fruit commands 15-20% premium

## Cotton - Telangana markets
- Price linked to moisture and trash content
- Ginning quality affects net realization

## Paddy - Andhra markets
- Parboiled vs raw paddy different rates
- Check MSP announcement for floor reference

## Selling tips
- Avoid distress sale during midday heat - quality degrades
- Transport during cooler hours for perishables
